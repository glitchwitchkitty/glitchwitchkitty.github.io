Kat Magyar

Curiosity is my medium.

Hello, I'm Kat. I'm an artist, tinkerer, pattern observer, and occasional chaos gremlin based in the Midwestern United States. I have many hobbies and even more curiosities. This website originally served as my student portfolio, but now it's a showcase of ✨ whatever I feel like ✨.

I've worn many hats and will continue to do so until I die or get bored. I make things, take things apart, fall down rabbit holes, and collect skills like little souvenirs from each adventure. Not everything I start gets finished, and not everything I learn needs to serve a greater purpose.

Life is too short to not explore the things that make you curious. This is a living archive of my curiosity, my creations, and the breadcrumbs I leave behind as I figure things out.

Thank you for stopping by. Hope you find something that sparks your curiosity!
