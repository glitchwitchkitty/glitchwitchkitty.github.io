current status

Building systems that support curiosity.

I'm Kitty — a data analyst, privacy advocate, and chaos engineer based in the midwest. The last few months have been an unexpected lesson in slowing down. Health challenges have shifted my routines and priorities, and I've been learning to work with the energy I have instead of the energy I want. My curiousity is still here, but I'm a little burnt out on health metrics. I'm finding a different rhythm, making room for creativity and remembering that not everything worthwhile has to be productive.

I do not have to keep carrying something just because I once did.

what i'm working on

Building things, braking things, documenting the chaos.

I've been spending more time exploring design, branding, and web development. I'm excited to say, this includes working with a very talented small business with their visual identity.

I've also been playing around with the idea of developing a creative studio built around experimentation, accessible tutorials, and sharing what I learn along the way. My current approach? Learn something, make something, document the process, and occasionally question what led me there.

what i'm learning

Curiosity doesn't follow a syllabus.

Lately, my interests have been bouncing between web development, digital design, privacy, AI, and the strange overlap between. I'm especially interested in making technical subjects more approachable, understanding the systems we depend on, and finding creative ways to teach through experimentation. I'm discovering that learning doesn't always need to lead to a cert, a career move, or a finished project. Sometimes understanding something is enough.

outside the terminal

A quieter kind of chaos.

Life away from the screen looks a little different these days. I'm making time for gentle movement, nighttime walks, creative projects, and the occasional unexpected rabbit hole.

I'm stilldrawn to plants, books, games, and far too many hobbies for one lifetime. Some projects are resting. Others are slowly finding their way back into rotation. Life is just more intentional and I kind of like it.

November means more craftings, cozy tea, and warm socks.

a few things i currently believe

Subject to revision.

  • Rest is not a reward for productivity
  • Curiosity deserves room to exist without becoming a career.
  • Privacy is a form of autonomy.
  • Documentation is an act of kindness to your future self.
  • Progress isn't always visible, measurable, or linear.
  • Sometimes the most useful thing you can build is a life that actually fits you.
